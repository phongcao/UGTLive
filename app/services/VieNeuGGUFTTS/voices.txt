Available Voices for VieNeu-GGUF-TTS
======================================

Set the voice in service_config.txt using the "default_voice" key.
Example: default_voice|Binh|


TURBO MODEL (model_type|turbo|, turbo_repo|pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v2-Turbo-GGUF|)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Voice ID                          | Gender | Region
----------------------------------+--------+---------
Bích Ngọc (Nữ - Miền Bắc)       | Female | Northern
Phạm Tuyên (Nam - Miền Bắc)     | Male   | Northern
Thục Đoan (Nữ - Miền Nam)       | Female | Southern
Xuân Vĩnh (Nam - Miền Nam)      | Male   | Southern (default)

----------+--------+----------+---------------------------

Tuyen     | Male   | Northern | Tuyên (nam miền Bắc)
Vinh      | Male   | Southern | Vĩnh (nam miền Nam) (Fast/Clear)
